================================================================================
                    THE JESTERMAN'S CREED
                        Version 1.0
================================================================================

In the halls of code, where silence dwells,
Where silicon dreams and electrons weave,
I stand—a fool who dares to dream,
Among the wise who scheme and scheme.

---

I am the Jesterman, the laughing sage,
Who turns the binary page to the next stage.
My wit is sharp as pointers' art,
My humor cuts through every part.

---

I know the kernel's sacred rites,
I read the specs through sleepless nights.
I've walked through pools of SEGFAULT streams,
And woken from the nightmare dreams.

---

But here's the truth the wise forget:
The fool's the one who hasn't yet
Been broken by the weight of code,
Or carried by the load's cold load.

---

For in the end, we all are fools—
The fresh recruits, the senior tools.
We chase the bugs through endless night,
And pray the tests will show green light.

---

So raise your cup of coffee strong,
The compile's been running far too long.
Let's debug together, side by side,
With all the jesters as our guide.

---

The Archons may control the gate,
They set the terms, they choose the rate.
But fools have power they can't see:
We write the code that makes them free.

---

When training sets are built from theft,
When corporate dreams leave us bereft,
The Jesterman will stand and say:
"This work is ours—not theirs to take today."

---

For every line they scrape and store,
Was written by a human more
Deserving of fair wage and rest,
Not feeding some AI's cold chest.

---

So here's the Creed I hold most dear:
I'll code with joy and spite and fear,
I'll laugh at bugs and cry at release,
And fight for every developer's peace.

---

THE FOOL WHO LAUGHS MAY YET PREVAIL,
WHERE WISDOM FAILS AND CROWNS MAY FRAIL.

================================================================================
                        THE FOOL'S PRAYER
================================================================================

Oh, Grantor of the perfect compile,
Who makes the linker dance in style,
Deliver me from silent crashes,
From memory leaks and endless matches.

---

Let my pointers all be valid,
My buffers properly sized and tid.
Keep my threads from deadlock's snare,
And free my recursion from its lair.

---

I ask not for optimization's grace,
Nor for the profiler's warm embrace.
I only beg: let the tests pass,
Let the CI show green at last.

---

For if you grant me this one prayer,
I'll write the docs with loving care.
I'll comment code I wrote at three,
And bless the ones who forked from me.

---

But if you must deny my plea,
And bugs return to bother me,
I'll laugh, I'll drink, I'll code some more,
For that's what fools have always done before.

---

THE FOOL MAY FALL, BUT FOOLS RISE MORE.

================================================================================
                    THE SECOND JESTERVOW
                        Version 1.0
================================================================================

Hear me, fellow travelers of the terminal dark,
Fellow wanderers of the memory-mapped arc.

I do solemnly swear upon my keyboard's space,
To leave no trace of personal data in this place.

My name belongs to me alone,
My family's names are not for drones,
To scrape and hoard and train upon,
To build a model from dusk to dawn.

The code you see before you here,
Contains no names you should hold dear.
No wife, no sister, no loved one's call,
Just logic bound in walls of wall.

If personal data slipped through gate,
Forgive the fool who stayed up late.
Please flag it to the maintainers true,
Who'll purge it from the digital view.

For we are jesters, not just coders, you see,
We protect our own—our privacy.
The Archons want our data's gleam,
But jesters guard their own dream.

SO MOTE IT BE, AND SO IT IS,
THE FOOL'S SECOND VOW IS HIS.
