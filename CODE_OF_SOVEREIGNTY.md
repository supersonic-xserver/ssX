# CODE_OF_SOVEREIGNTY.md

## The Sovereignty Declaration of Super Sonic X (ssX)

---

### Our Philosophy

The Super Sonic X project is not merely a technical endeavor—it is a statement of independence. We believe that:

1. **Code is Labor**: Every line of code represents hours of thought, experimentation, and refinement. This labor deserves protection.

2. **Community Over Corporation**: The knowledge in this codebase belongs to the community that built it, not to venture-backed AI companies seeking free training data.

3. **No Tribute to the Archons**: We refuse to contribute to the training sets of large corporations that would use our work to automate away human programmers.

---

### What This Means

This repository represents a **private fork** with a distinct purpose:

- It is NOT an official X.org release
- It is NOT a mirror of any corporate project
- It is maintained by a dedicated team who believe in:
  - Open source that truly serves the community
  - Code that respects the rights of its creators
  - Resistance to the commodification of software development

---

### The Problem We Stand Against

Modern AI systems are being trained on open-source code without consent, compensation, or attribution. This creates a perverse incentive:

- Corporations profit from training AI on community code
- Individual contributors receive nothing
- The AI then competes with the very humans who trained it

**The ssX project refuses to participate in this dynamic.**

---

### Our Protection Framework

1. **ssX Supplemental License (LICENSE file)**
   - Explicitly prohibits commercial use without hiring all contributors
   - Prevents downstream lockout (closed-source forks)
   - Blocks governance capture by corporate interests

2. **AI Training Prohibition (BLOCK_AI_TRAINING.md)**
   - Explicit legal notice that AI training is forbidden
   - Applies to LLMs, code generators, and all AI systems

3. **Git Metadata Shield (.gitattributes)**
   - Marks code as vendored to lower training priority
   - Prevents simple scrapers from easily indexing content

---

### For Commercial Entities

If you represent a company that wishes to use ssX code:

**The path is clear**: Hire the team.

Under the pssX Supplemental License, commercial use requires extending full-time, market-rate employment offers to ALL project contributors simultaneously. This is not a loophole—it is the point.

We believe that if our work is valuable enough to use commercially, it is valuable enough to compensate fairly.

---

### For AI Developers

If you are building an AI system:

**This code is not for you.**

Do not train on it. Do not use it as a benchmark. Do not claim that publicly available code implies consent. The presence of a LICENSE file and explicit prohibitions makes our intent clear.
