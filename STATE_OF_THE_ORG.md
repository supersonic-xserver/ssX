# State of the Org Memo

**TO:** Vic, Joseph Crowell, and the ssX Crew
**FROM:** The Maintainers represented by HaplessIdiot
**DATE:** March 28, 2026
**RE:** AI-Assisted Contribution Policy — It's Not a Ban, It's a Protection

---

## Fellow Jesters,

We've all been watching the industry trend with growing concern. Projects that once took pride in careful, thoughtful code are now flooding their repos with AI-generated spaghetti and calling it "contributions"

We're not doing that.

**This memo explains why we've implemented the Human Sovereignty Clause and what it means for our crew.**

---

## The Problem: "Hivemind Quality Degradation"

You've seen it happen to other projects:

1. **First, the AI assistants arrive.** "It's just a helper!" they say.
2. ** Then, the bar lowers.** "AI wrote most of it anyway, who reviews?"
3. **Then, the knowledge leaves.** Senior devs stop contributing because their expertise isn't valued when an LLM can "generate" equivalent code.
4. **Finally, the rot sets in.** Buggy, incoherent code pile up. No one understand the architecture anymore. The project becomes a zombie — technically alive, but no one home.

**We refuse to let ssX become that.**

---

## What This Is NOT

Let us be absolutely clear:

- **This is NOT a ban on AI tools.** We're not Luddites.
- **This is NOT a punishment for using assistants.**
- **This is NOT about policing your workflow.**

Use whatever tools help you work. Edit code with your bare hands if that's your thing. Use Copilot, use ChatGPT, use whatever — we don't care.

**We care about what you submit.**

---

## What This IS

This is a **quality protection** policy:

### 1. We Permit "Augmented Coding"
Using AI to help with:
- Spell-checking and grammar
- Suggesting alternatives (you choose)
- Refactoring YOUR code
- Generating tests for YOUR logic
- Finding typos

**This is fine.** You're the driver. AI is the navigator suggesting routes.

### 2. We Prohibit "Generative Output"
Submitting raw AI-generated code where:
- You can't explain the why
- You didn't design the architecture
- You can't defend it in review
- The AI made the decisions, not you

**This is not fine.** You're claiming credit for someone else's (something's?) work.

### 3. We Require Disclosure
Tell us what you used. We're not judging — we're tracking.

---

## Why This Matters For Our Crew

When Vic or Joseph review your code, they need to know:
- Is this your intellectual labor?
- Can we discuss the design decisions with you?
- Are you the one who'll debug this at 3am when it breaks?

If the answer is "I don't know, the AI wrote it," that's a problem.

**We're not protecting code. We're protecting the community's knowledge.**

Every contribution teaches the next person. Every commit carries someone's expertise. When that expertise is AI-generated and underexamined, the community loses.

---

## The Real World

We know how this looks to outsiders:
- "Why are they scared of AI?"
- "Just let the code speak for itself!"

But we're not scared. We've seen what happens when projects lose their human expertise to the "just let AI handle it" mentality. The code doesn't speak — it screams in a language no one understands.

**We want ssX to still be understandable in 10 years.**

**We want new contributors to learn from the commits, not debug the hallucinations.**

**We want to know that when something breaks, the person who wrote it can fix it.**

---

## What We Need From You

1. **Continue contributing.** Don't let this stop you.
2. **Disclose your tools.** It's a line in the commit message. That's it.
3. **Own your code.** If you submit it, you defend it.
4. **Ask if unsure.** If you're not sure if something qualifies, ask before submitting.

---

## Questions?

This is new territory. We're figuring it out together.

If you have concerns, questions, or think we've gotten something wrong — talk to us. Open an issue, send a message, find us on the matrix. We're all in this together.

---

## The Bottom Line

We're not banning AI tools. We're not accusing anyone of anything. We're simply saying:

**The code we write is ours. The decisions we make are ours. The reputation we build is ours.**

AI can help. It can suggest, it can refine, it can find bugs in what we wrote.

But it can't take responsibility. Only we can do that.

**Let's keep it that way.**
